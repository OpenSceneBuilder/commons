# Stewardship and decisions

OSB begins with Rene (@BIM4GIB) as its initial steward. Additional maintainers and a second organization owner will be named explicitly as the team forms.

## Responsibilities

- The organization owner manages identity, membership, account recovery and operating commitments.
- Each project maintainer owns scope, reviews, release readiness and project documentation.
- Contributors own the work they agree to undertake and should be credited for it.
- Significant decisions record their context, alternatives, outcome and responsible person.

## Decisions

Discuss public technical decisions in public issues or pull requests. Keep access records, sensitive reports and private project decisions in restricted spaces. A maintainer resolves routine project decisions; disputed organization decisions go to the steward with a written rationale.

Changing repository visibility, transferring projects, committing expenditure and selecting licenses are explicit stewardship decisions. Existing projects are not automatically moved into OSB.

## Earning responsibility

Responsibility grows through sustained contributions, sound review and care for other contributors. Maintainer appointments and access changes are recorded explicitly. No contributor is expected to provide unlimited unpaid support.

## Continuity

A second trusted owner, an access review cadence and a tested restoration procedure remain foundation work. Do not describe them as operating controls until they have been established and verified.
