# Working together

We welcome careful questions, different backgrounds and disagreement supported by evidence.

- Critique ideas and work without attacking people.
- Make room for people who are learning or approaching the problem differently.
- Respect privacy, attribution and the limits of someone's time.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate or publish someone else's private information.
- State uncertainty and limitations honestly.

The initial steward, Rene (@BIM4GIB), handles conduct concerns and may limit participation when needed to protect contributors. A dedicated confidential reporting route is still being established; do not put sensitive personal details into public issues. Until that route is published, use an existing private channel with the steward if you have one.
